{# The password, and the one fact about who used it last (ADR-013, AIG 14). Changing it ends every session, including the one submitting the form — which is why this page says so twice, once in the sentence and once in the redirect the route hands back. The alternative (keep the current session alive) would mean a credential that no longer works still authorises something, and that is the sort of gap an operator only discovers when they have changed a password because they wanted the old one to stop working. `last_login` is the entire history this appliance keeps about signing in: one timestamp, no address, no user agent, nothing about the guests who were queueing while it was set (ADR-007). #}
