{# The persistent region: what is on the speakers, and what is waiting (SAPRS 9.7, 9.8, 9.9). The `id` is the contract. `encore/api/fragments.py` names the region `player` and sends this markup under the frame `swap:player`; `encore/static/js/encore-live.js` looks the region up by that name and replaces this element. When the two disagree a panel stops updating — quietly, on one phone in the room — so the name appears in `tests/integration/test_htmx_pages.py` rather than only here. `aria-live="off"` is deliberate. A region that announces itself every second would talk over a screen-reader user's own navigation, and SAPRS 9.2's accessibility requirement is satisfied by the status region below it, which changes only when the appliance has a sentence worth hearing. #}
